msfcore

A small programmable chip that plays, watches and breaks digital protocols: 16 bits, four threads, one cycle an instruction, on IHP's 130 nm process through Tiny Tapeout. New protocols are firmware. This page runs the project's own assembler, deadline checker, ISA model and protocol learner in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Loading Python, about 10 MB the first time.

Certify a program

Choose a program or paste your own, in the instruction set's assembly. The checker follows every path and either proves that no WAIT_CMP is ever late, or refuses. Try the first example at BIT=9: too few cycles a bit for its own loop.

Learn a protocol from a recording

Give it a recording of one wire nobody documented. It works out how the bits are coded and what the frames hold, from the timing alone, refuses what fits no code, and writes firmware that sends a message the transmitter never sent. The firmware has to pass the checker above.

The examples are synthetic, made from each code's specification by tools/site_build.py. A real run through the chip's own logic analyser is in the project's tests.
